Short Answer Question Format
– Questions will generally give a recommended number of 
words/paragraphs for your answer.
– Each question will specify the number of marks.
Example
Question X (5 marks)
Describe the key characteristics of a DDOS attack.
– Consider a complex attack scenario where it might be exploited. 
– Consider what can lead to this attack.
– Consider how it will impact the stakeholders, such as in a long-term 
consequences.

Coding
– You will not be asked to write code during the exam.
– You will not be asked to test code during the exam.
– You should be familiar with the overall web development 
process, both front-end and back-end.
– You may be asked questions about Django as a 
framework or the features it provides (ORM, User 
module, templating). This is more about the concepts 
than any specific implementation detail.
