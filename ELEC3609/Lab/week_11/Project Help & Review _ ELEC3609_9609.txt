# **ELEC3609/9609 - Week 11 Lab** 

## **Project Help & Review Session** 

There is **no in-person project demonstration** in this lab. The session is reserved for teams to review their Assignment 2 work and ask tutors for help before submission. 

Use the lab time to: 

- clarify the Assignment 2 requirements and submission process; 

- ask questions about implementation, testing, documentation, or the recorded demonstration video; 

- troubleshoot remaining technical issues with guidance from a tutor; 

- review whether your report and video provide sufficient evidence for the features you implemented; and 

- prepare for the Week 12 technical interview. 

### **INFO** 

Bring your current source code, report draft, and a list of specific questions. Tutors can provide guidance and feedback, but the session is not an assessed presentation and tutors will not complete or pre-mark the assignment for you. 

For the deliverables and submission deadline, refer to the Assignment 2 requirements.
