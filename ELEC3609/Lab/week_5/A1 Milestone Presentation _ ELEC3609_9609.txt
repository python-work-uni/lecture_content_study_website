# **ELEC3609/9609 - Week 5 Lab** 

## **A1 Milestone Presentation** 

Each group will give a short pitch of their proposed project idea to the class and tutors. 

## **Task Description** 

In this milestone presentation, each group will deliver a **5-minute elevator pitch** introducing the **core concept and innovation** of their proposed website project. 

The goal is to impress the audience with the **merits and potential** of your future web application. Focus only on the **core value** of the project, what makes it interesting, unique, and valuable to your target users. 

After each pitch, there will be a **brief Q&A session** with tutors and possibly other teams, offering valuable feedback and ideas for improving your project. 

## **Presentation Order and Time Allocation** 

Groups will present in **ascending group number order** (e.g., Group 1 → Group 2 → Group 3…). 

### **INFO** 

Please be ready two groups ahead of your scheduled turn. If your group needs to present **earlier due to urgent circumstances** , please notify the tutors **before the lab begins** . 

Each group has **5 minutes** for the presentation, followed by a short Q&A.
