# **ELEC3609/9609 - Week 12 Lab** 

## **A2 Technical Interview** 

Each group will participate in a technical interview conducted by the tutors, focusing on A2 your source code, system architecture, and development process. 

## **Task Description** 

This lab session is a **technical interview** to assess your understanding of your implementation (Assignment 2). Tutors will visit each group and ask questions about: 

- Your **project architecture** and design choices. 

- **Code structure** and key implementation decisions. 

- The content in your submitted **assignment report** . 

- Any technical challenges or trade-offs encountered. 

Please be prepared to **explain, justify, and walk through** parts of your system using your submitted materials from **Week 11** (i.e., source code and report). 

## **Presentation Order and Time Allocation** 

Groups will be interviewed in **ascending group number order** (e.g., Group 1 → Group 2 → Group 3…). 



### **INFO** 

If your group needs to be interviewed earlier due to urgent circumstances, please notify the tutors **before the lab begins** .

## **During Interview** 

- Groups will not present to the whole lab. Tutors will **visit your table** and conduct the interview **individually** . 

- Ensure your group has: 

   - A **running version of your project** (local only). 

   - Your **assignment report** and **source code** ready to access. 

- Be respectful and quiet while other groups are being interviewed. 

- All group members must attend. Everyone should be ready to answer. 

## **Reminder** 

This interview contributes to your final project mark and focuses on your **technical understanding and individual contributions** . To prepare: 

- **Practice explaining** your system architecture and logic. 

- Ensure your app runs correctly and key features are accessible. 

- Use your report and submitted codebase to support your answers.
