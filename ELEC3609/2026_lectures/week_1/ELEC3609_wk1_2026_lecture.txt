SLIDE 13: Web Sites & Identifiers
================================================================================
Web Sites:
- A website is a collection of web pages on the World Wide Web.
- A single web page can be identified by a Uniform Resource Locator (URL). This is also known as a Uniform Resource Identifier (URI).
  - protocol://domain.tld/path/file.ext
  - https://google.com
  - mailto:jiawen.wen@sydney.edu.au
  - ftp://ftp.google.com
- Web sites are generally made up of HTML, JavaScript, and CSS and can contain multiple pages and hyperlinks.

SLIDE 36: Laboratory Safety Rules
================================================================================
Rules in the labs:
- Students must follow all and any reasonable safety instructions from the tutors/lab demonstrators.

Laboratory Safety Rules & Requirements:
1. Exposed open footwear (such as sandals) or bare feet are not permitted within laboratory areas. Solid, non-slip footwear must be worn at all times.
2. Food and drink are not to be consumed or placed on the benches at any time within a laboratory.
3. Students or Users must store their bags under the benches at all times.
4. Students or Users should clean and tidy up when they have finished and, before they leave the laboratory, return any material that they've been handed for use during the session.
5. Under no circumstance is the main laboratory power switch to be switched on by students. The laboratory emergency stops should be used in case of an emergency only to cut power to the laboratory.
6. Any faulty equipment is to be reported to the laboratory officer.
7. Tampering with, or removal of, any laboratory equipment is strictly forbidden.
8. Students or Users are expected to conduct themselves in a reserved manner and to keep noise at a minimum at all times; the laboratory is a teaching and learning environment.
9. Work that is not specifically associated with a timetabled Unit of Study may only be carried out with the prior approval of the Technical Staff or other staff members authorised to do so.
10. If you need to make or take a call, please step outside. Entertainment devices must not be used within a laboratory.
11. Students are not permitted to work alone in any laboratory at any time.
12. No one who is intoxicated by alcohol or prescription or non-prescription drugs is allowed to be in a laboratory.
13. Personal protective equipment such as protective eyewear must be worn for all operations and at all times, as specified in the School's safe operating procedures (e.g., when soldering, prototyping circuits, using hand or power cutting tools, or near others doing so).

SLIDE 47: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
================================================================================
FAQs:

Q: What kind of skills would be "must-have" for this course?
A: Some programming skills are highly regarded. They don't have to be in JavaScript/Python but you would find it useful if you have used them before; More importantly, you will have to get your hands dirty in the end.

Q: What is the process of grouping?
A: Details will be covered during the lab sessions. Selection & forming criteria include proximity and experience. Voluntary pairing is also considered.

Q: Can I change my group in the middle?
A: No. Once grouping is finalised and in play, it will not be changed.

Q: Is attendance mandatory?
A: I strongly recommend you attend all sessions, lecture and labs. If you miss one session, some vital information might not be available to you and I cannot guarantee the information is repeated on the slide deck. Attendance is not a direct indicator of your performance, but you need to attend all sessions to make sure you achieve the best outcome possible and maximise the value of this course.
